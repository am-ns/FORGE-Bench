\section{Experiments}

\subsection{Experimental setup}

We evaluate ten image-to-video systems: HunyuanVideo 1.5, HunyuanVideo 1.5 Distill, MiniMax Hailuo 2.3 (API model identifier: \texttt{MiniMax-Hailuo-2.3}), MiniMax H3, Kling 3.0 Standard, Seedance 2.5, CogVideoX 1.5, Wan 2.1, Wan 2.2, and Wan 3.0. Each system receives the same 500 reference-image/task pairs and the frozen generation-facing prompts. Videos are mapped by task identifier and decoded under a common evaluation pipeline.

The formal judge is Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B-Instruct-FP8, served with four-GPU tensor parallelism. Four deterministic data shards are evaluated concurrently. Seedance 2.5 contains 499 valid videos; task \texttt{erob\_190} is explicitly recorded as a rejected model output and remains in coverage accounting.

\subsection{Limitations}

The benchmark is finite and does not cover every industrial sector, geographic standard, or equipment type. Some physical properties cannot be inferred reliably from pixels alone. Multimodal judges can be biased or inconsistent even under deterministic decoding, and contact-sheet evidence compresses temporal information. The operational split is balanced by domain rather than by real deployment frequency. Finally, benchmark scores indicate conformance to annotated visual contracts, not certification of safety, engineering correctness, or legal compliance.
